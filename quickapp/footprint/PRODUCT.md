# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Football players operating a 432 x 514 px REDMI Watch 6 during a match, often while moving and with only a brief glance available. During the competition stage, judges also need to reproduce the complete workflow reliably in the OpenVELA simulator.

## Product Purpose

LynxTracker records a football match on the watch, keeps the recording safe while offline, and makes the few in-match controls fast and difficult to misuse. The current milestone is a complete, deterministic simulator experience; real sensor and phone-link adapters remain a later hardware-validation stage.

## Brand Personality

Focused, restrained, athletic. The interface should feel calm under pressure, precise about state, and immediately legible on an AMOLED watch.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative gradients, glow, shadows, glass effects, densely stacked controls, solid-filled danger actions, and confirmation modals for reversible actions. Do not make read-only information look tappable.

## Design Principles

1. Make the current match state readable at a glance.
2. Give each screen one obvious primary action and separate dangerous actions visually.
3. Let taps perform reversible actions; reserve a deliberate hold for ending the match.
4. Preserve recording data and timing across navigation and recovery paths.
5. Keep simulator evidence distinct from future real-device validation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Status is communicated with text as well as color. Text and controls use high contrast on the near-black AMOLED background, touch targets remain large, and the UI avoids decorative motion or effects that reduce legibility.
